⚠️ The "Linear" Blindspot
Pearson Correlation only measures linear association.

Look at the graph. X and Y are perfectly related by a U-shaped curve, so X determines Y exactly.

Yet their Pearson Correlation is 0.00 (computed from these points), because the symmetric curve has no linear trend: the least-squares line is flat, sitting at the average height ȳ. Never trust r without looking at your data!
